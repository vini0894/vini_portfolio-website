Case 03 · Funnel Optimisation

Rebuilding the path from sign-up to first booking.

Plenty of people signed up and never booked a first session. I found the one step where intent leaked, and tested the fix instead of redesigning everything.

45→82% booking conversion, validated against a matched control group.

01 · Problem

Intent was there. Conversion wasn't.

Conversion to first booking sat at 45%, and no single step obviously explained the loss. The funnel looked fine in aggregate, which is usually a sign the problem is hiding inside one stage.

02 · Method

Map it. Instrument it. Find the one leak.

I mapped the end-to-end journey, instrumented every step, and ran cohort and funnel analysis to locate exactly where intent dropped. Then I A/B tested fixes on the highest-leverage step rather than rebuilding the whole flow, so the win was attributable and safe.

1step carried most of the drop-off
A/Btested against a matched control
37ptabsolute lift in conversion

03 · Result

Conversion nearly doubled.

04 · Decision

A repeatable lens for activation.

The approach became the default for activation work: map the journey, find the leak, test the smallest change that fixes it, ship what the data backs. Big redesigns rarely beat one well-aimed fix.
